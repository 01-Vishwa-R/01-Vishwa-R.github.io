NutriBuddy

Empathetic Real-Time AI Nutritionist with Clinical RAG

Clinical dieticians struggle to maintain continuous daily accountability for dozens of clients between appointments. NutriBuddy bridges this gap using low-latency Gemini Live voice interaction, with instant recall over patient medical charts.

The Problem

Patients fall off diets due to unanswered daily queries, while dieticians face communication burnout.

The Solution

Real-time bidirectional voice assistant backed by vector embeddings of medical records and an administrative clinician dashboard.

Clinical Nutrition Intelligence with Zero-Hallucination Guardrails
